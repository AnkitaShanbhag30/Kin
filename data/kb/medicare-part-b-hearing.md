# Original Medicare (Part A & B) and Hearing Aids

- Original Medicare does not cover hearing aids or exams for fitting hearing aids.
- Medicare Part B covers diagnostic hearing and balance exams when a doctor or other provider orders them to see if medical treatment is needed. The member pays 20% of the Medicare-approved amount after the Part B deductible.
- Medicare Advantage (Part C) plans may offer extra hearing benefits such as routine exams, hearing aid copays, or OTC allowances. Coverage varies by plan; check the plan's Evidence of Coverage.
- Hearing aids are a qualified medical expense for HSA and FSA funds. People enrolled in Medicare cannot contribute new money to an HSA, but can spend an existing HSA balance on hearing aids.
- Source basis: Medicare.gov "Hearing & balance exams" and "Hearing aids" coverage pages.
