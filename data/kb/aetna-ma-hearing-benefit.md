# Aetna Medicare Premier (HMO) — Hearing Benefits Summary (DEMO PLAN DOCUMENT)

> Demo document for the Kin hackathon prototype. Structure modeled on typical Medicare Advantage hearing benefits. Dollar figures are illustrative, not Aetna's published terms. Replace with the member's real Evidence of Coverage before real use.

## Routine hearing exam
- $0 copay for one routine hearing exam per calendar year with an in-network provider (NationsHearing network).

## Prescription hearing aids (through NationsHearing)
- Covers up to 2 prescription hearing aids (one per ear) every 3 years.
- Member pays a fixed copay per hearing aid by technology tier: Standard $499 per ear, Advanced $799 per ear, Premium $1,099 per ear.
- Requires a hearing exam and fitting with a NationsHearing network provider. Includes a 60-day trial period and 3 follow-up visits.
- Hearing aids bought outside NationsHearing are not covered under this prescription benefit.

## Over-the-counter (OTC) hearing aid allowance
- The plan reimburses up to $250 per ear ($500 per pair) every 3 years toward FDA-authorized OTC hearing aids purchased from any retailer.
- To claim: submit the itemized receipt and the OTC Hearing Aid Reimbursement Form within 90 days of purchase. Reimbursement is paid by check within 30 days of approval.
- Personal sound amplification products (PSAPs), sound amplifiers, and devices that are not FDA-authorized hearing aids are not eligible.
- Members may use either the prescription benefit or the OTC allowance in a 3-year period, not both.

## Not covered
- Replacement batteries, cleaning supplies, and extended warranties.
- Devices returned for a refund: the reimbursement must be repaid if the device is returned.
