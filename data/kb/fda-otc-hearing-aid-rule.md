# FDA Over-the-Counter Hearing Aid Rule (summary)

- The FDA final rule creating a category of over-the-counter (OTC) hearing aids took effect October 17, 2022.
- OTC hearing aids are for adults 18 and older with perceived mild to moderate hearing loss. They can be bought without a medical exam, prescription, or audiologist fitting.
- Prescription hearing aids are for severe loss, people under 18, or anyone whose needs are not met by OTC devices. They are fitted by a licensed professional.
- Personal sound amplification products (PSAPs) are not hearing aids. They are intended for people with normal hearing to amplify sound in certain situations and are not meant to treat hearing loss.
- "FDA registered" means a company registered its facility or listed a product. It does not mean the FDA cleared or authorized the device as a hearing aid.
- Red flags for caregivers: products marketed as hearing aids at very low prices, "FDA registered" as the only claim, and no stated hearing-loss range.
