# Kin Demo Merchant — Shipping, Payment and Returns (DEMO)

- Kin Demo Merchant is a simulated checkout used by the Kin prototype. No real purchase is made.
- Shipping: free standard shipping, delivered in 3 business days to addresses in the continental US. Signature not required.
- Payment: accepts HSA and FSA cards for FDA-authorized hearing aids, and the card on file.
- Itemized receipts are emailed at checkout and include the device's FDA category, which the OTC allowance reimbursement form requires.
- Returns follow the original retailer's window, counted from the delivery date. Prepaid return labels are included in the box.
